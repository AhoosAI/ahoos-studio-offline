## What's new in 3.4.0

**Colour and glass.** Grey with no tint and one accent, which is the text's own colour, in light and dark. The top bar, the sidebar, the message box and every menu are frosted glass over the page.

**A quiet first page.** A greeting and the message box, and nothing else.

**The message box in three places.**
- The **+** menu holds files, web search, the model's memory, temperature, and skills.
- One chip picks the mode: normal, plan or agents.
- The model picker moved from the top of the window down beside the send button.

Temperature fits on one line now.

All conversations stay in the studio's folder on your computer, as before.
